# A Field Guide to Reusable Water Bottles

The right bottle is comfortable to carry, simple to clean, and suited to the daily routine.

## Material Choices

Stainless steel keeps drinks cold, while lightweight recycled plastic can be easier for long walks.

## Size and Carrying

Choose a capacity that fits the bag and a lid that will not leak during a commute.

## Cleaning and Care

Rinse the bottle after use and check the seal regularly to keep it fresh and reliable.
