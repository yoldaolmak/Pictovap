# A Weeknight Vegetable Curry

This simple recipe turns fresh vegetables, spices, and rice into a dependable dinner.

## Prepare the Ingredients

Cut the vegetables into similar pieces and measure the spices before heating the pan.

## Build the Sauce

Toast the spices, add tomatoes and coconut milk, and let the sauce become fragrant.

## Finish and Serve

Stir in the vegetables until tender, then serve the curry with warm rice and herbs.
